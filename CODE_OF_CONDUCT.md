# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes:

- being considerate and constructive;
- assuming good faith while discussing technical disagreements;
- accepting correction and focusing on the work;
- respecting boundaries and confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainers through the private contact method listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
